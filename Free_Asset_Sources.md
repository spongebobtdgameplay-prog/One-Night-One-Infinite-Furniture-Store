## Quaternius

# Important style rule
Do not mix assets untouched if their art styles clash.
Our final target is a coherent semi-realistic horror-store look:
- recolor/retexture sourced models
- standardize roughness/metallic values
- standardize scale
- standardize lightmap/collision/LOD policy
